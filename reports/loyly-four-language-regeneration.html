Behavioral truth and sampled runtime diagnostics are deliberately different grades of evidence.

Browser / HTTP exchangeDeterministic with pinned fixtureAuthoritativeExact request, response, and status are replayable.
Domain eventDeterministic contentAuthoritative when contract declares itIdentity, type, aggregate, and content digest are validated.
Projection snapshotDeterministic contentAuthoritative when contract declares itComplete correlated views are countable.
Invariant resultDeterministic verdictAuthoritativeOracle evaluates the invariant; the app cannot author pass/fail.
Trace spanNon-deterministic timing/orderDiagnosticScheduling and exporter timing vary.
eBPF profile sampleNon-deterministic samplingExploratorySamples depend on load, interval, symbols, and runtime support.
Runtime diagnosticNon-deterministicExploratoryLogs explain collection state but do not prove behavior.
