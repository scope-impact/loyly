# Brf Masthugget: bastu and gym rules

Provenance record; raw artifact not captured, cite-only (see `README.md`).

- **URL:** https://brfmasthugget.se/bastu-och-gym/
- **Publisher:** Brf Masthugget, a bostadsrättsförening in Gothenburg, Sweden.
- **Tenure model:** Swedish bostadsrätt (cooperative ownership).
- **Language:** Swedish.
- **Verified:** fetched and read 2026-08-26.

## Key claims

- Weekday sauna hours are **gender separated**; weekends have **bookable family times**.
- A booking is a **1.5 hour pass**.
- There is an explicit **cap on family-time bookings**, verbatim: "Varje lägenhet kan
  endast ha en aktiv/kommande bokning på familjetiderna i bastun." (Each apartment can
  hold only one active or upcoming booking on the family times.)
- An **emergency access fee of 1,200 kr** applies (called out when access rules are
  broken).

## Role in the corpus

The richest single booking document found in the public sweep. It grounds three policy
variables the generic model must express: gender-separated recurring times, a distinct
bookable "family time" class, and a one-active-booking-per-apartment cap scoped to a time
class rather than to the whole sauna.
