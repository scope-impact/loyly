# Yle 2022-11-01: sauna fee levels and the reimbursement gap

Provenance record; raw artifact not captured, cite-only (see `README.md`).

- **URL:** https://yle.fi/a/74-20003099
- **Publisher:** Yle (Finnish public broadcaster), news article, 2022-11-01.
- **Language:** Finnish.
- **Verified:** fetched and read 2026-08-26.

## Key claims

- **Kela reimburses at most 10 EUR per month** for a sauna fee (in the benefit context the
  article covers).
- **Lumo** (a large rental landlord) **charges 20 EUR** per month.
- A housing company in **Akaa raised its fee to 25 EUR** per month.

## Role in the corpus

Real fee data and a real conflict: the fee a house sets and the fee an institution will
reimburse are different numbers set by different parties. Grounds the rule that fee amount
is per-cooperative policy, and that fee changes are contested events, not configuration
trivia.
