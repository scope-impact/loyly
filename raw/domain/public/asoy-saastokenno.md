# As Oy Säästökenno: saunavuorot

Provenance record; raw artifact not captured, cite-only (see `README.md`).

- **URL:** https://saastokenno.com/saunavuorot/
- **Publisher:** As Oy Säästökenno, a Finnish asunto-osakeyhtiö.
- **Tenure model:** Finnish asunto-osakeyhtiö (shareholder co-op, like Soukanvuori).
- **Language:** Finnish.
- **Verified:** fetched and read 2026-08-26.

## Key claims

- The company has **seven saunas**.
- Standing slots are **fixed weekly and bi-weekly** reservations.
- Booking goes **via the maintenance company**, not resident self-service.
- A **flexible-slot experiment was discontinued in 2023** for two stated reasons: **low
  usage and energy cost**.

## Role in the corpus

The closest public analogue to Soukanvuori (same tenure model, manager-mediated booking,
standing slots). The discontinued flexible experiment is the corpus's best documented
"why": a real cooperative tried looser booking and rolled it back because utilisation and
heating economics punished it. Any feature that loosens standing slots must answer this
precedent.
