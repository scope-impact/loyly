## Key claims

- **Section 5.2:** sauna use is **by contract**, with a **fee set by the unit board**.
- **Section 5.5:** the shared laundry (fellesvaskeri) runs on **tokens**, with usage hours
  **enforced by automatic power cutoff**.

## Role in the corpus

The Norwegian leg, which suits the client audience. It grounds two things: fee-setting as a
board power exercised per facility, and enforcement by automation rather than by social
pressure (the power cutoff is the laundry's equivalent of a booking-driven sauna heater).
