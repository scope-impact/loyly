# Suomen Kiinteistölehti: board authority over common facilities

Provenance record; raw artifact not captured, cite-only (see `README.md`).

- **Publisher:** Suomen Kiinteistölehti, Finnish property-management trade press.
- **Language:** Finnish.
- **Verified:** fetched and read 2026-08-26.

## Key claims

- Rules for a housing company's **common facilities are agreed in the board**
  (hallitus): the board is the authority that sets and revises them.

## Role in the corpus

Grounds the authority model: whatever a booking system enforces, the rules it enforces are
board decisions, and the board can change them. The system is downstream of governance,
never the other way around.
