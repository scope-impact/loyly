# Soukanvuori: the first real configuration

These are real working documents from **As Oy Soukanvuori** (Espoo), transcribed from
Sami's own Gmail. Soukanvuori is Sami's housing company, and by the decision of 2026-08-26
it is the product's first real configuration: the deliberately awkward case that every
abstraction has to survive (see the repo README).

## Nothing here is invented

An earlier iteration of this project used invented placeholder documents, plausible
enough to fool a reader, and rejected them for exactly that reason (INV-1 in
`docs/wow/invariants.md` tells the story). Nothing of the kind remains: every quoted
sentence in these files is copied from a real 2022 email thread, and the citation on
each says which message it came from.

## Redaction policy (applied to every file here)

Scrubbed, because readers point their own AI agents at this repo: resident and named
manager-staff names, phone numbers, email addresses, the apartment and door number, the
street address, and the property's Kiinteisto id. What survives is the rules, which is the
only part the project needs. Two names are deliberately KEPT because they are not private
in the relevant sense and the domain story needs them:

- **Soukanvuori**, the housing company, kept by the 2026-08-26 decision as the named first
  configuration.
- **Braleva Kiinteistopalvelut**, the property manager, kept as the manager actor. Its
  individual customer-service staff are redacted to `[asiakasneuvoja]`.

The work-order number **103016** is kept: it is not personal data, and it is part of the
domain evidence (the same booking carried an id across three systems that then drifted).

## Why this source is irreplaceable

The public corpus (Leg 2, the thirteen verified brf/borettslag/taloyhtio documents) grounds
the general model and shows the variation across cooperatives and countries. This mail is
the only source for how ONE real cooperative actually operates, including the parts nobody
writes into house rules: that booking is mediated by the manager rather than self-service,
that sauna days differ per dressing room inside one stairwell sauna, and that a confirmed
booking updates three representations that can drift out of sync.
