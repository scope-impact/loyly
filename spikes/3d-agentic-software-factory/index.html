Löyly Factory is the reusable embodied regeneration system. This essay follows one example product—the Löyly booking behavior—but the factory can build and measure other approved product behaviors. How 01 · Direction and route

Choose the life before it begins

Pi explores intent and presents an exact selection for human approval. Candidate history never chooses the operation: the operator explicitly requests Generate or Regenerate, and ORC receives an immutable route before work begins.

No parent · no accepted product oracle

Fresh Slice Generation begins with no parent Candidate and no accepted product oracle, regardless of repository history.

How 03 · Independent Grade

The judge is designed after the Candidate—but without seeing it

After verification, Grade creates the independent truth boundary. In fresh generation, a new external author sees only the approved cut and public calibration protocol. ORC calibrates and seals the result before it can observe the Candidate.

Before the builder

Harness snapshot

A fresh immutable build of committed harness code, runtime assets, public application contract, calibration fixtures, and generic execution machinery. It is calibrated locally and hidden from the builder.

Inside Grade

Cycle oracle

A provisional candidate-blind bundle containing the behavioral grader and only applicable affordance and runtime-conformance evaluators, sealed to the cut, selection, snapshot, and author model.

After a complete pass

Oracle proposal

Normalized successful evidence bound to scenario digests, Candidate subject, judge, denominator, and its own stable digest. It is still non-authoritative.

After exact human approval

Product oracle

Manually ratified expected evidence published as an immutable OCI descriptor. Later measured cycles compare fresh evidence with its selected entries.

The oracle is not a grader. It is the manually approved catalogue of normalized expected evidence. The cycle oracle is the provisional judging bundle; the proposal is the approval candidate; the product oracle is the ratified reference. A failed Grade cannot teach or repair the Candidate—it may begin only another clean sample from the approved cut.

Canonical contract, incomplete runtime

The merged behavioral model defines this authority chain. The current runtime still treats accepted product OCI material as executable grader assets and does not yet complete proposal approval, evidence-oracle publication, explicit Generate/Regenerate routing, or full cycle-preparation approval. Those are reconciliation obligations—not alternate definitions.
