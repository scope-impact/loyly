Body

An LLM is a brilliant brain. A harness gives it a body capable of learning the truth.

Löyly is not the factory. It is one behavior the factory must express. Pi and ORC are the machinery that lets creative inference see, act, fail safely, be judged independently, and remember what happened.

01 · Anatomy

A complete body, not a larger brain

The architecture becomes easier to understand when every part has a biological job. The harness is the organism; Löyly is one learned behavior expressed through it.

The key distinction: the harness is not another product. It is the machinery that lets many products—or many expressions of one product—be born, observed and improved honestly.

03 · The Phoenix loop

Failure changes the next body

The Candidate is not secretly repaired after judgment. Evidence changes the direction, and the corrected direction produces a child generation. This preserves a clean causal chain from intent to implementation to truth.

Creativity remains free inside the boundary. Judgment remains independent outside it. Memory connects lives without contaminating them.
